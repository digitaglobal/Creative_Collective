# Staff calendar

Switch Calendar/List from the Operations booking filters. Calendar opens to the selected date (or today) in Jamaica time. Day and Week modes have Previous, Today and Next controls. Week starts Monday; days stack on small screens and use columns on larger screens.

Existing booking filters apply. The date field anchors the displayed day/week instead of discarding bookings starting on other dates. Bookings spanning several days appear on each overlapping day. Cancelled, expired and no-show bookings are hidden until explicitly included. Tapping a booking opens the existing staff details and actions.

Resource-specific and all-space closures appear as blocked-time cards, filtered by selected resource and venue. Empty days do not guarantee availability. Capacity, shared allocations, opening hours and booking rules remain authoritative in the booking service.

Validation: typecheck, production build, and mobile calendar browser test passed. Tested day/week navigation, seven-day range, Today, opening booking details and horizontal overflow; mobile screenshot reviewed.
